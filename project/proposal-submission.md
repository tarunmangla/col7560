## Proposal Submission

To ensure that you begin your course project with a clear plan, each project group must submit a short project proposal describing the project they intend to work on and how they plan to approach it.

The purpose of this proposal is to help you:
- understand the scope of the project,
- break the work into concrete steps,
- identify datasets, tools, and models you will use, and
- plan a realistic timeline for completing the project.

Your proposal should be concise (up to 2 pages) and include the following components.

### 1. Project Selection
The project title you are selecting. A list of project ideas have been provided in the `project-ideas.md` file, but you are also welcome to propose your own project idea. If you choose to propose your own idea, please discuss with me before submitting the proposal to ensure that it is appropriate for the course.

### 2. Proposed Understanding and Approach
Describe how you plan to solve the problem. This should include information such as data source, features, machine learning models, evaluation plan (metrics, baselines, comparisons).

Your approach does not need to be perfect at this stage, but it should show that you have thought through the pipeline.

### 3. Project Plan and Timeline
Break the project into major milestones. For example:
- Week 1–2: Literature review and dataset exploration
- Week 3–4: Feature extraction pipeline
- Week 5–6: Model training and baseline implementation
- Week 7: Evaluation and analysis
- Week 8: Report writing and final improvements

The timeline should be realistic.
